FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How long does onboarding take?

Most teams are up and running within 15 minutes. Connect your repository, and LABSKY auto-detects your framework and configures the build pipeline. For enterprise setups with custom configurations, our team provides dedicated onboarding support.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes. Our Starter plan is free forever with 3 projects and generous usage limits. Pro and Enterprise plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

What kind of support do you offer?

Starter plans include community support via our forums and documentation. Pro plans get priority email support with 24-hour response times. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 dedicated support with a named account manager and custom SLAs.

Can I migrate from my current provider?

Absolutely. We provide migration guides for all major platforms and offer hands-on migration assistance for Pro and Enterprise customers. Most migrations complete in under a week with zero downtime.
