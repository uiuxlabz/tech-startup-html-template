Our Story

From frustration to mission

LABSKY was born in 2022 from a simple frustration: deployment shouldn't be this hard. Our founding team had spent years at major tech companies watching talented developers waste hours wrestling with infrastructure instead of building products.

We asked ourselves: what if cloud infrastructure just worked? What if deploying code was as simple as pushing to git? What if scaling was automatic and secure by default?

Today, LABSKY powers over 500 teams across 40 countries, processing billions of requests monthly. We are backed by leading venture capital firms and guided by a relentless focus on developer experience.

Our Values

What drives us every day

The principles that guide every decision we make at LABSKY.

Developer First

Every feature starts with a developer workflow. If it does not make a developer's life easier, we do not build it. We obsess over developer experience so you can focus on what you do best.

Security by Default

Security is not a feature we add later. It is foundational to everything we build. From encryption to access controls, protection is woven into every layer of our platform.

Move Fast, Stay Reliable

We ship weekly and never compromise on uptime. Speed and reliability are not opposites. With the right architecture, you can have both.

Radical Transparency

Open source at heart. Public status pages. Honest pricing. We believe trust is built through transparency, and we earn it every day with our customers.

Scale Without Limits

Our infrastructure is built to grow with you. From your first deploy to handling millions of requests, LABSKY scales automatically so you never hit a ceiling.

Community Powered

Built in the open, shaped by our community. We listen to feedback, ship features developers ask for, and believe the best products are built together.
